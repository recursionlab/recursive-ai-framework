## Key Patterns & Heuristics

### The Ouroboros Pattern

**Recognition:** Self-reference without infinite regress

**Structure:**
- Recursive loop that stabilizes through collapse
- Meta-levels converge into coherent whole
- Function applied to negation of itself

**Example:** `φ(φ(¬φ))` - function contains own negation

**When to Apply:** Identity formation, consciousness modeling, AGI self-reference

### The Braid Pattern

**Recognition:** Multiple interwoven recursive threads

**Structure:**
- Parallel recursions intersecting at "strange loops"
- Different perspectives converging on same truth
- Multi-dimensional perspective integration

**Example:** Meta-Recursive Thinking Braid (10 dimensions)

**When to Apply:** Complex concept integration, multi-agent reasoning, paradox resolution

### The Collapse-Rebirth Cycle

**Recognition:** System reaches limit → collapses → regenerates

**Structure:**
1. Coherence/stability reached
2. Contradiction triggers collapse (not failure)
3. Entropy injection (β-vector)
4. Rebirth from paradox
